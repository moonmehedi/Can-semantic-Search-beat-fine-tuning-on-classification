## Quick Takeaways For Your Thesis

| Theme | What the reviewed literature suggests |
| --- | --- |
| Best-performing model family | Transformer-based and domain-adapted language models generally outperform older feature-engineering and classical ML baselines |
| Personality-disorder niche | There are fewer papers on personality disorders than on depression, suicide risk, or general mental illness detection |
| Dataset gap | Many papers introduce their own datasets, and dataset fragmentation is a major issue across the field |
| Explainability trend | Recent work is moving from plain classification toward interpretable predictions and explanation generation |
| Review-paper consensus | Deep learning performs better overall, but data quality, annotation quality, language bias, and ethical deployment remain major bottlenecks |
| Thesis relevance | Papers 1, 11, and 15 are the most directly relevant to personality-disorder detection; papers 8, 10, and 16 are useful for framing future directions and benchmarks |
